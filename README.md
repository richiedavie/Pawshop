🐾 What is PawShop?
PawShop is a all-in-one mobile application designed to simplify life for pet parents. It bridges the gap between pet profile management, shopping for pet supplies, and pet care services.

🛠️ Key Features & Capabilities
Personalized Pet Profiles:

Allows users to create dedicated profiles for their pets (dogs, cats, small animals).

Stores details like pet name, species, age, and avatar images to tailor recommendations.

Pet E-Commerce & Marketplace:

Browse and purchase pet products (food, toys, accessories, grooming supplies).

Feature-rich shopping experience with product listings, search/filters, and a seamless checkout flow.

User Account & Customization:

Dual-identity system where users manage both their human profile (contact info, address, avatars) and their individual pets' profiles.

Care & Services (Potential Integration):

Tracks pet-specific needs, order history, or recurring purchases (e.g., monthly food subscriptions).

🎯 Target Audience
PawShop is tailored for pet owners and pet enthusiasts looking for a clean, convenient way to manage their pets' needs and buy high-quality pet products directly from their smartphones.
